Common Questions

Frequently asked
questions

Do I need to provide any collateral or security?

No. Our loans are entirely unsecured. We do not require property, machinery, gold, or any other asset as security. Eligibility is based solely on your business turnover and financial health.

How is the interest rate determined?

Rates are competitive and determined based on your business turnover, cash flow patterns, and loan tenure. Because we're a direct lender, there's no broker markup — you get better rates than most intermediary-routed channels.

How long does approval actually take?

Once all documents are submitted, approval decisions are typically made within 24–48 hours. Disbursal follows within 1–3 working days total. We move at the speed your business needs.

Is my business eligible if I don't have a great credit score?

We evaluate eligibility primarily on business turnover, GST filings, and bank statements — not credit scores. Many of our clients have been funded after being turned down by banks for credit-related reasons.

Can I repay early without penalty?

Early repayment terms are discussed clearly at the time of loan structuring. We believe in complete transparency — there are no hidden charges or surprise penalties. All terms are clear before you sign.

What states and cities do you serve?

We primarily serve Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Pondicherry, Karnataka (Urban Cities), and Hyderabad - Telangana. Contact us if you're in a nearby area — we evaluate on a case-by-case basis.
